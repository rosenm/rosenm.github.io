## The Language Project {#language-project}

**Links:** [Article](https://indiabioscience.org/columns/indian-scenario/the-journey-of-the-language-project) · [Videos](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCSYv2GGrFXr4G4x6coUGKlg/featured)

The Language Project is a multilingual science communication initiative aimed at making biology and synthetic biology education accessible beyond English-speaking audiences.

The project produced more than 100 educational videos across 26 languages through collaborations with multiple international iGEM teams and was recognized with a nomination for the iGEM 2019 Best Education and Public Engagement Award.

Related coverage:
